# QA sweep of 11 November 2026

Nine walkers drove the 804 cases in docs/qa/cases against a local copy of the app in a browser, one role at a time (w1 to w4 Admin, w5 Department Head, w6 SBO Officer, w7 SAO, w8 Student, w9 General). Their results are in D:/Claude/scratch/hiusa-1111/rubric/w1 to w9/results.json and the triage is in all-failures.json.

## Totals

560 cases passed. 158 failed. Of those 158, 88 were stale cases: the app deliberately behaves differently from what the case said (wording changed, a flow was simplified, a feature was retired), so the case was wrong and not the app. The other failures are real defects or unsure items and are being fixed separately. 21 cases could not be run on a local copy. 42 cases were not reached in the time available. The few cases left over (partial or informational verdicts) are not counted in these four groups.

## What was done about the stale cases

All 88 stale cases were rewritten in docs/qa/cases so that the title, steps and expected result describe the app as it is now. Ids, roles, modules and priorities were kept. Where a case described a retired feature it was kept, its title now ends with "(not part of the system)" or says the feature is retired, and its expected result starts with "Not part of the system:" and gives the reason. The retired features are the New User form in People, the Department Head's Register an organization form (replaced by Allow a student to apply and the student's own application), the public registration portal, the Review registration drawer, the Agency overview, and the officer view of AI delegation.

The walkers' results show 22 more cases with a stale verdict that the triage did not list (SAO-014, 015, 016, 019, 022, 034, 035, 036, 045, 046 and GEN-012, 020, 021, 035, 037, 042, 075, 130 to 134). They were rewritten the same way, so 110 cases changed in all. The generated sheets (docs/qa/lean/*, docs/QA_TEST_SHEET.csv and docs/QA_TEST_SHEET.xlsx) were rebuilt from the JSON with scripts/build-qa-lean.mjs and scripts/build-qa-sheet.mjs.
