OPENING PITCH (WHAT TO SAY AT THE START OF THE PRESENTATION)
================================================================================
"Good morning panel. Today we are presenting HIUSA — an integrated institutional
governance, financial transparency, event lifecycle, and AI decision-support
platform built for university student organizations.

HIUSA enforces a strict 3-tier institutional governance model:
  1. Student Organization Level (Organization Admins, SBO Officers, and Students)
  2. College Level (Department Heads / College Deans who endorse their college's
     organizations, budgets, events, and financial reports)
  3. University Level (The Student Affairs Office / SAO, which governs academic
     terms, accreditation compliance, campus venues, final approvals, clearances,
     and student grievances).

Every query in the system is isolated by organization_id and role boundaries, and
every role features a Hybrid Semantic Search & Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
Ask AI Copilot that synthesizes live database records into formatted HTML tables,
progress meters, and visual CSS graphs."
