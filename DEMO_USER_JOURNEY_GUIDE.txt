"Good morning panel. Today we are presenting HIUSA — an integrated institutional
governance, financial transparency, event lifecycle, and AI decision-support
platform built for university student organizations.

HIUSA enforces a strict 3-tier institutional governance model:
  1. Student Organization Level (Organization Admins, SBO Officers, and Students)
  2. College Level (Department Heads / College Deans who endorse their college's

progress meters, and visual CSS graphs."

ROLE-BY-ROLE SPEAKER CHEAT SHEET (WHAT EACH ROLE IS & WHAT IT CAN DO)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE 1: SAO / SUPER ADMIN (Student Affairs Office — University-Wide Authority)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- WHO THEY ARE:
  The highest institutional authority in HIUSA (e.g., SAO Director). They oversee
  every academic college and every accredited student organization across the
  entire university.
- WHAT TO SAY TO INTRODUCE THIS ROLE:
  "The SAO / Super Admin is the university-wide regulator. Instead of managing a
  single club's day-to-day tasks, the SAO governs the entire institutional
  ecosystem—controlling academic terms, accrediting organizations, giving final
  sign-off on college-endorsed events and financial reports, managing campus
  venues, resolving student grievances, and signing final student clearances."
- WHAT THIS ROLE CAN DO (CAPABILITIES):
  * Open and close university Academic Years and Semesters.
  * Review new student organization applications submitted by College Deans and
    provision the initial Organization Admin (President) account.
  * Define university-wide accreditation requirements (PDF templates, deadlines)
    and approve or return organization compliance submissions.
  * Give final institutional approval on budgets, events, and semester financial
    reports after the College Dean endorses them.
  * Manage campus venues and approve/reject event room booking requests.
  * Triage and resolve student grievances ranked by AI urgency classification.
  * Sign the final (3rd) stage of student clearances and audit system security logs.
  * Ask the AI Copilot university-wide operational questions across all colleges.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE 2: DEPARTMENT HEAD / COLLEGE DEAN (Academic College Endorser)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- WHO THEY ARE:
  The head of a specific academic college (e.g., Dean of the College of Computer
  Studies). They act as the middle governance tier between student organizations
  in their college and the university-wide SAO.
- WHAT TO SAY TO INTRODUCE THIS ROLE:
  "The Department Head or College Dean represents the academic college layer.
  They only see and govern the student organizations belonging to their own
  college. Nothing from a student organization can reach the SAO without first
  passing through the Dean's endorsement inbox."
- WHAT THIS ROLE CAN DO (CAPABILITIES):
  * Register new student organizations under their academic college and submit
    registration documents to the SAO.
  * Review, endorse, or return budgets, event proposals, and semester financial
    reports from organizations within their college (Stage 1 Approval).
  * Monitor accreditation compliance progress for all organizations in their college.
  * Sign the 2nd stage of student clearances (after the student organization clears
    the student, and before final SAO sign-off).
  * Use the College-scoped Ask AI Copilot to check which organizations in their
    college have or haven't submitted requirements or financial reports.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE 3: ORGANIZATION ADMIN / PRESIDENT (Student Organization Chief Executive)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- WHO THEY ARE:
  The top student leader (President / Organization Admin) of a specific accredited
  student organization (e.g., PSITS-CCS). Their access is strictly isolated by
  `organization_id` to their own organization.
- WHAT TO SAY TO INTRODUCE THIS ROLE:
  "The Organization Admin—typically the Student Organization President—is the
  chief executive of a single student organization. They have full control over
  their organization's internal operations: members, officers, AI task delegation,
  event proposals, budgets, accounting ledgers, merchandise store, elections, and
  accreditation uploads."
- WHAT THIS ROLE CAN DO (CAPABILITIES):
  * Manage the organization's member directory, assign SBO Officer positions, and
    enroll student biometric fingerprints for event attendance.
  * Delegate tasks to officers using the AI Task Delegation Engine (which scores
    officers by position fit, workload, completion rate, and recency).
  * Create event proposals, book campus venues, upload event requirement files,
    and open post-event student evaluations.
  * Manage the full Financial Suite: propose semester budgets, record income/expense
    ledger transactions, issue student membership fee invoices, track cash advances
    and liquidations, run AI Financial Forecasting, and submit financial reports.
  * Manage the organization's Merchandise Catalog, stock levels, and promos.
  * Create and manage organization Elections, ballot positions, and candidates.
  * Upload mandatory accreditation compliance documents to the SAO.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE 4: SBO OFFICER (Student Body Organization Officer — Operational Executor)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- WHO THEY ARE:
  Elected or appointed officers of a student organization (e.g., Vice President,
  Secretary, Treasurer, Auditor, PIO). They handle day-to-day execution inside
  their organization without having destructive administrative privileges.
- WHAT TO SAY TO INTRODUCE THIS ROLE:
  "The SBO Officer role is built for day-to-day operational execution. While the
  President sets up budgets, elections, and compliance, SBO Officers like the
  Treasurer or Secretary focus on executing assigned tasks, scanning attendance
  at events, verifying student merchandise payments, and clearing students at
  the organization level."
- WHAT THIS ROLE CAN DO (CAPABILITIES):
  * View assigned tasks on the Task Board, update completion percentages (0-100%),
    and log progress notes.
  * Operate the live Event Check-In terminal using biometric fingerprint scanning
    or manual School ID verification.
  * Verify student payment proofs (e.g., GCash reference numbers) for merchandise
    orders, approve orders, and mark items as claimed.
  * Sign or place holds on Stage 1 (Organization-level) student clearances.
  * Post organization announcements and use the Officer Ask AI Copilot to check
    overdue tasks, pending orders, and upcoming events.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE 5: STUDENT (General Campus Member — Self-Service Portal)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- WHO THEY ARE:
  Every enrolled student member of an organization. Their account is strictly
  scoped to their own personal academic, financial, and clearance records plus
  the public activities of their organization.
- WHAT TO SAY TO INTRODUCE THIS ROLE:
  "Finally, the Student role provides complete self-service transparency for
  every campus member. Instead of lining up at multiple offices to ask why their
  clearance is on hold or how much they owe in organization fees, a student can
  track their 3-stage clearance, view their Statement of Account, buy org
  merchandise, vote in elections, evaluate events, and ask their personal AI
  assistant about their own status."
- WHAT THIS ROLE CAN DO (CAPABILITIES):
  * Track their 3-stage clearance progress (Organization -> Dean -> SAO) in real
    time and see exact hold reasons or unpaid obligations.
  * View their personal Statement of Account (open fee invoices, payment history,
    and official receipts).
  * Browse their organization's Merchandise Store, place orders, upload payment
    proofs, and track order fulfillment.
  * Cast a secure, one-student-one-vote ballot in active organization elections.
  * View upcoming organization events, check their attendance logs, and submit
    post-event feedback evaluations.
  * Submit confidential or formal student grievances to the SAO.
  * Ask their Personal AI Assistant questions strictly scoped to their own
    clearance signatories, unpaid fees, orders, and announcements.
