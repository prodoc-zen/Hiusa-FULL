# HIUSA V2 - SAO Administrator Cheat Sheet

Welcome to the **HIUSA Student Organization Management System**. This guide provides a quick overview of how to navigate the platform as a Student Affairs Office (SAO) Administrator.

## 🔑 1. Logging In
As the SAO Super Admin, you have university-wide visibility. 
1. Navigate to the login portal.
2. Select **SAO** as your organization.
3. Use your provided credentials (or the Demo Quick Fill button).

## 🏛️ 2. The University Dashboard
Upon logging in, you will see the **University Dashboard**. This gives you a bird's-eye view of:
- **Organizations:** Active and inactive student organizations across the university.
- **Financial Summary:** Real-time tracking of total income, expenses, and net balance across all budgets.
- **Recent Events:** The latest upcoming or ongoing events across all organizations.

## 📝 3. Managing Organizations
1. Click **Organizations** in the sidebar.
2. Here you can view every registered student organization.
3. You can click into any organization to view their specific officers, members, events, and financial ledgers.

## 💳 4. Financial Oversight
1. Click **Finance & Budgets** in the sidebar.
2. SAO has the unique ability to review the budgets of *all* organizations.
3. You will see line-item transactions and can verify if organizations are spending within their approved limits.

## 📢 5. University-Wide Announcements
1. Click **Announcements**.
2. You can post announcements that will appear on the dashboard of **every single student and officer** in the university.
3. Use the "Priority" flag to pin critical announcements (e.g., "Annual Audit Deadline").

## 🛡️ 6. Audit Logs
1. Click **Audit Logs**.
2. This is an SAO-exclusive feature. It tracks every significant action taken in the system, such as event approvals, budget modifications, or role changes.
3. Use this for transparency and compliance reporting.

Enjoy streamlined organization management!
