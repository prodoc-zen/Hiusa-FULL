# Anti-slop audit 001

Date: 2026-09-18
Mode: AFTER

## Design read

- Product: HIUSA organization governance application for students, officers, administrators, department heads, and Student Affairs.
- Direction source: `design.md`.
- Identity constraints: HIUSA logo, Poppins typography, Lucide icons, compact institutional layouts, and the documented blue, cyan, navy, neutral, and semantic status palette.
- Dials: ENERGY 1, RHYTHM 2, MOTION 1.

## Findings

1. HIGH, R-25: Muted text and several blue or cyan text pairings did not meet WCAG AA contrast.
2. HIGH, R-03: Several dashboard and module summaries started at two columns on phone layouts.
3. HIGH, R-03 and R-32: Multiple icon controls were smaller than the minimum touch target.
4. HIGH, R-26 and R-32: Hand-built dialogs did not consistently provide Escape handling, focus trapping, scroll locking, or focus restoration.
5. HIGH, R-27: Some request failures were represented as legitimate empty data.
6. HIGH, R-02: User-facing text contained em dashes.
7. MEDIUM, R-14: Role dashboards reused the same equal-weight metric-card composition without role-specific hierarchy.
8. LOW, R-11: Panel radii drifted from the 8px direction in `design.md`.
9. LOW, R-29: Structural colors included undocumented near-duplicate blues, navies, and purple accents.

The user approved all findings for remediation while keeping the HIUSA palette, Poppins typography, branding, workflows, routes, and API behavior.
